Kulu Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Kulu (“the App”) is an online shopping application. This policy explains what data we collect and how it is used.

1. Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a stable account identifier. We use this to create your Kulu account, authenticate API requests, and (if your email is designated as the store admin) grant admin access.

2. Orders and shipping

When you place an order we store product selections, quantities, prices, and the shipping name, phone, and address you provide, so we can fulfill and support your order.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Chapa. We store payment status and transaction references needed to confirm orders. We do not store full card numbers on Kulu servers.

4. Messages

Support chat messages between customers and the store admin are stored to provide customer service.

5. Device notifications

With your permission, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token to send notifications about orders and chat messages.

6. How we share data

We share data with service providers only as needed to operate the App: hosting, Firebase (authentication and messaging), and Chapa (payments). We do not sell your personal information.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to admin tools is restricted to authorized store accounts on the server.

8. Contact

For privacy requests, contact the store at the support email listed on the Google Play listing.
